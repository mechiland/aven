## Chinese typography: why 8.5, and why no higher

The current SC/TC native text is larger, readable and balanced at all four scales. Full Places labels reduce the strained, abbreviated feel of stock. Ordinary file names, Latin extensions, Chinese dates and punctuation stay clear. The SC and Taiwan/Hong Kong prose samples preserve stable wraps, open dense strokes and sensible punctuation/Latin spacing. There is no conspicuous whole-window fractional blur, missing-glyph box or clipped sample glyph in the inspected matrix.

The paired browser paragraphs already look good in stock. Aven does not demonstrate a dramatic rasterization improvement in that controlled specimen, whose 17 px / 1.75 reading rhythm is shared by both guests. The concrete product gains are native UI size, application integration, readable text preview and mail spacing. In the matched 720-pixel composer, successive wrapped lines and signature lines now sit roughly 30 pixels apart, versus about 24 in stock. That visible fix brings writing into line with the comfortable reading surface and resolves the round-03 objection.

The active native breadcrumb is still conspicuously dark. It prevents a higher score; it does not justify globally thinning otherwise balanced Noto text. Mail and preview body comparisons are at 1×; the full four-scale matrix covers Dolphin and Firefox. No direct macOS comparison, physical-panel reading study or every-language-content claim was performed.

## Remaining visible rough edges

1. Native active breadcrumbs are too heavy; Gwenview adds a prominent surrounding frame.
2. Thunderbird truncates sender addresses in its narrow cards and leaves a mostly empty upper strip.
3. Gwenview filmstrip bevels, gradient cells and its horizontal bar remain less refined than Files. The strip also costs fitted-photo area.
4. Firefox repeats the page title in its additional decoration and loses some content height.
5. Preview is functional and significantly better, but its keyboard interaction could be more discoverable and its separate task icon is visible during use.

These are nonblocking at the first focused prototype threshold and keep the score near 8 rather than 9–10. They do not require redesigning the rest of KDE.
